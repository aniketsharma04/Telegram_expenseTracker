# Indian regulatory and legal framework for a credit-card recommender that earns referral/affiliate income (for "Aaj", an SMS-reading expense tracker)

Scope note: research current to 1 Nov 2026. This is information, not legal advice. Primary RBI PDFs were partly blocked (captcha). Several findings come from close secondary reproductions (TaxGuru carries the full RBI text) and law-firm/compliance summaries. Each one is marked. Claims a lawyer must confirm are flagged "LAWYER".

## 1. RBI card directions: marketing, DSAs/DMAs, recovery, unsolicited cards, co-branding, network choice, KFS, and bank liability for agents

### Takeaway

The 2022 Master Direction has been replaced by the **RBI (Commercial Banks – Credit Cards and Debit Cards: Issuance and Conduct) Directions, 2025** (28 Nov 2025, in force immediately). The bank alone decides whether to issue a card. A DSA/DMA may only solicit and service customers. The bank is liable for its agents' acts and must limit the customer data it shares with them. A **June 2026 amendment package on advertising, marketing and sale (effective 1 Jan 2027)** widens "DSA/DMA" to any non-employee engaged to "sell, market, promote or influence" customers, "irrespective of contractual designation". That very likely covers a paid affiliate or lead-gen app (LAWYER).

### Cited Findings

- Title RBI/DOR/2025-26/155; DOR.AUT.REC.No.74/24-01-041/2025-26, dated 28 Nov 2025. Para 2: "shall come into force with immediate effect". Para 93 repeals the earlier card directions for commercial banks, as listed in circular DOR.RRC.REC.302/33-01-010/2025-26 (28 Nov 2025). — [TaxGuru reproduction of RBI Directions 2025](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html)
- Para 11(10): the final decision to issue a card stays with the card-issuer. DSAs/DMAs are limited to soliciting and servicing customers and accounts. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html)
- Para 11(9): telemarketers must follow TRAI rules and the NCPR (Do-Not-Call) framework, and may contact customers only between 10:00 and 19:00. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html)
- Paras 5, 11(4), 11(5): unsolicited cards and upgrades are "strictly prohibited". If an unsolicited card is billed, the issuer reverses the charges and pays a penalty of twice the amount. The issuer bears losses from misuse of an unsolicited card. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html)
- Paras 11(8) and 34: consent "shall be explicit and shall not be implied". Issuance needs written consent, or digital consent with multi-factor authentication. Unilateral upgrades and credit-limit increases are not allowed. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html)
- Para 11(1): a one-page Key Fact Statement must go with the application. Para 92 sets staged MITC disclosure: at marketing, at application (KFS), in the welcome kit, on bills, and on any change of terms. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html)
- Paras 38–40: on assigning a recovery agent, the issuer gives the customer the agent's name and contact. Agent communications must carry a senior officer's contact. Intimidation and harassment are prohibited. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html)
- Para 44: "The card-issuers shall also be liable for the acts of their agents." Para 42: outsourcing must not compromise service quality or risk management. Para 60: issuers remain liable for co-branding partners' acts. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html)
- Paras 41, 62, 86, 89: data shared with DSAs/DMAs/recovery agents must be limited to what they need. Card data goes to outsourcing partners only where essential and with explicit cardholder consent. Card data storage and ownership stay with the issuer. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html)
- Paras 61–62 (co-branding): the partner may only market or distribute cards and give access to goods and services. It may not access transaction data or take part in post-issuance processes, except as first grievance contact. Transaction data shown on its platform "shall neither be accessed nor be stored by the CBP". — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html)
- Issuers must make sure DSAs, DMAs and recovery agents do not transfer or misuse customer information while marketing cards. — [search summary of the 2025 Directions, TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html)
- **Card network choice:** RBI circular CO.DPSS.POLC.No.S1133/02-14-003/2023-24, 6 Mar 2024. Issuers must not sign network arrangements that stop them using other networks. Eligible customers get a choice of networks at issue. Existing cardholders get the option at next renewal. The circular took effect six months from its date (about Sep 2024). Issuers with 10 lakh or fewer active cards, and issuers on their own authorised network, are exempt. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/news/story/rbi-announces-new-guidelines-for-the-issuance-and-usage-of-credit-cards-check-details-420278-2024-03-06); [Taxmann](https://taxmann.com/post/blog/rbi-directs-card-issuers-to-provide-customers-with-an-option-to-choose-from-multiple-card-networks-at-time-of-issue); [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2024/03/223-card-issuers-to-allow-customers-to-choose-card-networks-rbi/) (notes it was unclear whether all five networks or just two must be offered)
- The 2025 Directions text extracted from TaxGuru did not show the network-choice clause. It may have been carried into another consolidated direction (payments side). Not verified. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html)
- **June 2026 "advertising, marketing and sale" amendments:** issued 15 Jun 2026 (press release 2026-2027/460), effective 1 Jan 2027. There are two sets per regulated-entity class. One amends Responsible Business Conduct. The other amends Undertaking of Financial Services (agency/referral). For commercial banks these are the Second Amendment (circular index Id 13485) and the Third Amendment (Id 13495). They cover banks of all kinds, NBFCs and HFCs. — [CorpLawUpdates summary](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/rbi-tightens-rules-advertising-marketing-sale-financial-products-regulated-entities); [RBI circular index Id 13485](https://rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx?Id=13485) (RBI/2026-27/115, DOR.MCS.REC.No.94/01-01-032/2026-27, 15 Jun 2026)
  - Para 4(10B) DSA/DMA: any entity or individual other than the bank's own employee that the bank engages to sell, market, promote or influence customers for its own or third-party products, "irrespective of the contractual designation or nomenclature used". It expressly includes Loan Service Providers. Para 4(10C) adds sub-agents. — [CorpLawUpdates](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/rbi-tightens-rules-advertising-marketing-sale-financial-products-regulated-entities)
  - Banks must publish their DSA/DMA list on their website and update it within 7 calendar days. Each DSA/DMA signs a Code of Conduct undertaking. Agreements must set penal or disciplinary action for violations. Agents must be visibly distinguishable from bank staff and must not pose as bank staff (Paras 85B, 85C, 85E, 85F, 85O). — [CorpLawUpdates](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/rbi-tightens-rules-advertising-marketing-sale-financial-products-regulated-entities)
  - Explicit consent means a specific, informed, unambiguous indication. The default must be "No / I do not agree". Consent records are kept for 1 year after the contract ends. Promotional messages need explicit consent, unsolicited push notifications are barred, and unsubscribing must be easy (Paras 4(13A), 85G–85I, 85L, 85M). — [CorpLawUpdates](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/rbi-tightens-rules-advertising-marketing-sale-financial-products-regulated-entities)
  - Eleven dark patterns are banned (false urgency, basket sneaking, confirm shaming, forced action, subscription trap, interface interference, bait and switch, drip pricing, disguised advertisement, nagging, trick wording; Annex IIA). The directions reference the CCPA 2023 Dark Patterns Guidelines and require user testing and periodic audits. — [CorpLawUpdates](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/rbi-tightens-rules-advertising-marketing-sale-financial-products-regulated-entities)
  - Mis-selling (Para 4(20A)) includes selling an unsuitable product even with consent, and selling with incomplete or misleading information. Suitability must be assessed (Para 85P). If mis-selling is established, the customer gets a full refund plus compensation (Para 85Z). TCCCPR/DND applies (Para 85ZA). — [CorpLawUpdates](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/rbi-tightens-rules-advertising-marketing-sale-financial-products-regulated-entities); [Moneylife on Feb 2026 draft](https://www.moneylife.in/article/bank-misselling-rbi-draft-rules-bar-incentives-forced-bundling-and-dark-patterns-by-banks/79655.html)
  - A Vinod Kothari explainer on the draft ("From Consent to Compensation") exists. — [Vinod Kothari PDF](https://vinodkothari.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/From-Consent-to-Compensation_-RBIs-Draft-Directions-for-REs-on-Sales-Practices.pdf)

### Inferences

- A product that recommends cards and is paid per application or approval is "engaged by a bank to … market, promote or influence customers". From 1 Jan 2027 it will most likely be treated as a DSA/DMA, whatever the contract calls it ("affiliate", "referral partner", "lead-gen"). Expect banks, or the affiliate networks in between, to push down: a Code of Conduct undertaking, listing on the bank's DSA page, consent-capture standards, no-dark-pattern UI, audit rights, data-minimisation and no-retention clauses, penalties and termination, and indemnities. This is because the bank is liable for agents' acts (Para 44). LAWYER: confirm whether a sub-affiliate under an affiliate network (Cuelinks/EarnKaro style) is a "sub-agent".
- The app may: show comparisons, explain features and fees, link to the bank's own application journey, and show the KFS/MITC that the bank supplies. The app may not: decide or imply approval, collect card applications and hand them to the bank without the bank's DSA framework, pre-tick consent boxes, push unsolicited promotional notifications, or use false urgency like "only today", pre-approved claims it can't back up, or nagging.
- Co-branding rules (no access to transaction data) apply only if Aaj ever launches a co-branded card. A pure referral is not co-branding.

## 2. Outsourcing and Digital Lending Directions 2025: are card lead-gen apps LSPs, DSAs or DMAs?

### Takeaway

The **RBI (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025** (8 May 2025) define an LSP as an agent of a regulated entity (RE) that carries out any part of the RE's digital lending functions, including customer acquisition. Commentators disagree on whether credit cards are outside these Directions. The safest reading is that card lead-gen is governed mainly by the card directions plus the 2027 DSA/DMA rules, and that the DSA/DMA definition now expressly includes LSPs. LAWYER.

### Cited Findings

- LSP definition: "An agent of a RE (including another RE) who carries out one or more of RE's digital lending functions, or part thereof, in customer acquisition, services incidental to underwriting and pricing, servicing…" — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/financial-services/1628380/reserve-bank-of-india-digital-lending-directions-2025); [AZB update](https://www.azbpartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/AZB-Update-Digital-Lending-Directions-2025.pdf); [RBI document](https://website.rbi.org.in/documents/d/rbi/directionsondigitallending)
- Sources conflict on credit card scope. SCC Online says only credit-card EMI programs are excluded and other card loans are in scope. Other commentary says credit cards and revolving credit, which have separate regimes, are excluded. — [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/05/14/rbi-digital-lending-directions-legal-news/); contrasted with [NLIU CBCL analysis](https://cbcl.nliu.ac.in/contemporary-issues/analysing-rbis-digital-lending-directions-2025-a-positive-step-towards-responsible-lending/)
- The 2027 DSA/DMA definition expressly includes LSPs, so the LSP label does not exempt an entity. — [CorpLawUpdates](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/rbi-tightens-rules-advertising-marketing-sale-financial-products-regulated-entities)

### Inferences

- If Aaj ever recommends personal loans, BNPL or "card-on-EMI" offers, the Digital Lending Directions plausibly apply. Aaj would then have LSP duties (RE disclosure, the RE listing it as an LSP, and the CIMS listing of DLAs) and Google Play's personal-loan policy would apply (see section 6). Keep the recommender to cards only, at least at first.

- Primary RBI text on the credit-card carve-out was not read. Outsourcing directions were not reviewed.

## 3. Credit bureau access: can a non-regulated app pull CIBIL/Experian scores?

### Takeaway

Not directly. Becoming a CIC "Specified User" needs an Indian company with net worth of at least ₹2 crore, 3 years' experience processing information for credit institutions, diversified ownership and CISA audits. That rules out a small independent app. The realistic routes are: (a) a bureau's consent-based consumer product under a contract with heavy restrictions (no onward sharing, data kept 6 months, stored in India, annual IS audit), or (b) asking users to fetch their own free annual report. Onward sharing of bureau data with banks is prohibited.

### Cited Findings

- **RBI (Credit Information Companies) Directions, 2025**, RBI/DOR/2025-26/378, 28 Nov 2025, in force immediately. — [TaxGuru reproduction](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-credit-information-companies-directions-2025.html)
- Specified User eligibility (para 14(3)): Indian company or statutory corporation; objects that allow processing information for credit institutions; CISA-certified IT systems; no moral-turpitude or economic-offence convictions; net worth of at least ₹2 crore on a continuing basis; owned and controlled by resident Indians; well-diversified ownership (promoters at most 26%, others at most 10%); at least 3 years' clean track record processing information for credit institutions. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-credit-information-companies-directions-2025.html)
- Para 14(4)(vi): "Sharing of credit information by SUs with any other person (including credit institutions) is in violation" of CICRA s.17(4). SUs may keep the data for at most 6 months. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-credit-information-companies-directions-2025.html)
- Para 24 (CIC sharing with entities under consumer consent): the CIC does due diligence first. The entity uses the report "solely for the limited end use as agreed", "shall not use or sell or resell or pass on information to any other person", and gives no access to group companies. Data is stored up to 6 months or until consent is withdrawn, processed and stored in India, and subject to an annual CISA audit. Consent must be prior, written, documented, verifiable and permanent in nature. Violations can lead to termination. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-credit-information-companies-directions-2025.html)
- Para 21(7): every CIC gives one Free Full Credit Report with score (300–900) once a year on request, linked from the CIC homepage. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-credit-information-companies-directions-2025.html)
- History: in 2019 RBI stopped banks and NBFCs giving unregulated entities access to bureau data. — [Business Standard 2019](https://www.business-standard.com/article/companies/fintech-firms-in-a-spot-after-rbi-restricts-access-to-credit-data-119092800985_1.html). Fintech access paths are explained in [Ikigai Law](https://www.ikigailaw.com/article/94/access-to-credit-bureau-information-for-fintechs).

### Inferences

- For Aaj, "check your score" features would have to run through a bureau or partner contract with the para-24 restrictions. The score could inform on-device recommendations, but must not be passed to banks. A simpler route is to let users enter their score band themselves.

### Gaps

- Not verified: current commercial terms and eligibility for Experian, CRIF or CIBIL consumer-score APIs for small apps. Not verified: how the 2025 Directions treat these "consent-based" partners in detail beyond para 24.

## 4. Account Aggregator: can a non-FIU app get users' bank data?

### Takeaway

No, not as an FIU. Only entities regulated by RBI, SEBI, IRDAI or PFRDA can be FIUs. An unregulated app can only (a) partner with a regulated FIU that receives the data and builds on top, or (b) act as a technology service provider, which does not make it the licensed party. Aaj's existing SMS-parsing approach sits outside the AA framework.

### Cited Findings

- Only entities registered with and regulated by RBI, SEBI, IRDAI or PFRDA may be FIUs (vendor summary). — [Setu docs](https://docs.setu.co/data/account-aggregator/licenses-and-go-live/participants-in-aa); [UnaCores FIU onboarding](https://unacores.github.io/unaport-docs/fiu_onboarding)
- A TSP does not need an NBFC-AA licence and builds FIU/FIP modules for regulated institutions. — [Sahamati TSP page](https://sahamati.org.in/tsp/fip-fiu-tsp)
- An unlicensed fintech has two options: get a licence, or partner with a regulated FIU. A TSP cannot be the licensed participant. Sahamati was reportedly recognised as the AA SRO on 5 Jun 2026 (unverified). — [eCorpIT blog](https://ecorpit.com/account-aggregator-integration-fintech-builders-2026/)
- RBI has registered 17 AAs, and 126 institutions were live as both FIP and FIU as of 30 Sep 2025. — [Ministry of Finance DFS](https://financialservices.gov.in/account-aggregator-framework)

## 5. DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025: timelines and obligations

### Takeaway

The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 Nov 2025 and phase in. Board provisions took effect immediately. Consent-manager registration starts 13 Nov 2026. Most data fiduciary duties (notice, consent, security, breach reporting, rights) and penalties start **13 May 2027**. Maximum penalties are ₹250 crore for security failures and ₹50 crore for other breaches. Aaj would be a Data Fiduciary for SMS-derived data. Sharing a user's profile with a bank or affiliate network needs separate, specific, purpose-limited consent.

### Cited Findings

- Notified 13 Nov 2025 (gazette 14 Nov per one source). Board provisions immediate. Consent Manager provisions (Rule 4) after 12 months, i.e. 13 Nov 2026. Most Data Fiduciary obligations after 18 months, i.e. 13 May 2027. — [ConsentOS timeline](https://consentos.in/learn/dpdp-compliance-timeline/); [TCSA roadmap](https://www.tcsa.in/resources/dpdp-rules-2025-implementation-roadmap); [Consently](https://www.consently.in/blog/dpdp-rules-2025-implementation-timeline-india); [Macksofy](https://www.macksofy.com/blog/dpdp-rules-2025-compliance-deadlines)
- Using a consent manager is optional for most businesses. Registration is mandatory only for entities that operate as consent managers. — [Vinsys](https://www.vinsys.com/blog/dpdp-act-compliance-deadline-nov-2026-for-consent-manager)
- Penalties: up to ₹250 crore per instance for failing to keep reasonable security safeguards, up to ₹50 crore for other provisions. Penalty provisions commence 13 May 2027 per one source. — [Digimart summary](https://www.digimartinfotech.in/dpdp-rules-2025-deadlines-penalties-guide.php); [Invield](https://www.invield.com/resources/dpdp-rules-checklist)
- One source (Glocert) gives a conflicting schedule and says the Rules were notified in Jan 2025, which was actually the draft date. Treat it as unreliable. — [Glocert](https://www.glocertinternational.com/resources/guides/dpdp-rules-2025-compliance-timeline/)
- RBI's 2027 sales-practice rules separately require explicit, default-"No" consent for promotional messages. — [CorpLawUpdates](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/rbi-tightens-rules-advertising-marketing-sale-financial-products-regulated-entities)

### Inferences

- Design now for May 2027. Use a stand-alone consent for "use my transaction data to recommend cards", separate from core expense tracking. Use another stand-alone consent for "share my name and phone with Bank X". Keep the recommender on-device where possible, so the bank or affiliate only receives a click or referral ID. Honour withdrawal and erasure. The bank is likely its own Data Fiduciary for application data. Aaj may act as a processor only where the bank's journey is embedded.

### Gaps

- Not sourced here: the exact Act and Rule sections on children (verifiable parental consent under 18), Significant Data Fiduciary thresholds, the 72-hour breach report to the Board, and the precise notice content. Check the MeitY gazette text.

## 6. Google Play: SMS permission and financial-services policies

### Takeaway

A non-default-SMS budgeting app can request READ_SMS/RECEIVE_SMS only under the "SMS-based money management" temporary exception. It needs a Permissions Declaration, the use must be core functionality, and it must not sell or share non-financial or personal SMS. Monetising SMS-derived insights through bank referrals is a risk: Play forbids any "transfer that results in a sale of this data". Personal-loan features would bring in the India licensing gate and permission bans.

### Cited Findings

- SMS and Call Log permissions are for default handlers. Temporary exceptions are allowed where "there's currently no alternative method to provide the core functionality", "subject to Google Play review and approval". — [Play Console Help: SMS/Call Log](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10208820?hl=en)
- Exception "SMS-based money management" ("apps that track and manage budget"): READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS, RECEIVE_MMS, RECEIVE_WAP_PUSH. There is a separate "SMS-based financial transactions" exception (UPI, transaction verification). — [Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10208820?hl=en)
- Conditions: the permission must enable "critical core functionality". Developers declare it in the Play Console Permissions Declaration Form. "Any transfer that results in a sale of this data (including SDKs that sell this data)" is an invalid use. Personal-loan and budgeting apps may not share non-financial or personal SMS history. — [Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10208820?hl=en)
- Personal-loan policy (India): only apps on RBI's digital-lending list, or those facilitating licensed lenders with disclosed partners, may be listed. Loan apps, lead generators and loan calculators are barred from READ_CONTACTS, photos, external storage, precise location and similar. The expansion to lines of credit had an enforcement deadline moved to 27 Aug 2025. — [PaisaPress](https://paisapress.com/blog/can-loan-app-access-contacts-india/); [Credolab guide](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/63036d38271cd821b954a407/68ec7962800abd3787068c8c_Credolab%20-%20Guide%20to%20Google%20Play%20Personal%20Loans%20Policy%20changes.pdf); [MyAppMonitor](https://myappmonitor.com/blog/google-play-personal-loans-policy-update); [ASO World](https://asoworld.com/blog/april-2025-google-play-policy-updates-key-changes-for-news-and-personal-loans-apps/)
- Enforcement: Google removed more than 2,000 Indian loan apps in 2022 and later acted on more than 3,500. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/08/25/google-personal-loan-apps-play-store-india-update/embed/); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/technology%2Fpolicy-violation-google-takes-enforcement-action-on-3500-loan-apps-on-play-store-in-india-1213589.html)

### Inferences

- The safe pattern: parse SMS on-device for budgeting (the core function). Derive coarse signals locally, such as "spends ₹X/month on fuel". Send no SMS content off-device to any partner. Update the Play Data Safety form and Declaration if recommendation becomes a stated use. Avoid any loan or EMI offers unless prepared for the personal-loan policy.

## 7. Advertising: ASCI, CCPA dark patterns and misleading ads, TRAI

### Takeaway

Paid placements and affiliate links must be clearly labelled (ASCI). Finance influencers or content need credentials only when they give investment or technical advice. Dark patterns are banned under the CCPA's 2023 Guidelines and, from 2027, under RBI's own list. TRAI TCCCPR/DND applies to any calls or SMS. Card advice is not SEBI investment advice, but don't drift into recommending investments.

### Cited Findings

- ASCI influencer guidelines Addendum 2 (7 Apr 2025, announced 28 Apr 2025) covers health and finance creators. Finance influencers giving investment advice must be SEBI-registered and show the number. General information needs no qualification. Approved disclosure labels include "ad", "sponsored", "collaboration", "partnership" and "affiliate". Advertisers share responsibility. — [Exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/advertising-news/asci-updates-influencer-guidelines-with-respect-to-health-finance-influencers-142977.html); [Conventus Law](https://conventuslaw.com/report/india-asci-updates-influencer-guidelines-for-health-and-finance-sectors-strikes-balance-between-expertise-and-expression/); [Angel One](https://www.angelone.in/blog/asci-eases-rules-for-financial-and-health-influencers-but-sebi-regulations-still-apply)
- In FY2024–25, 14% of ads ASCI processed were influencer violations, and only 29% of reviewed influencer posts fully complied with disclosure rules. — [Exchange4media 2025 review](https://www.exchange4media.com/influence-zone-news/2025-the-year-regulation-caught-up-with-influencer-marketing-150389.html)
- CCPA June 2025 advisory: platforms to self-audit for dark patterns within 3 months. One outlet reports the advisory extended to fintechs (single source). — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/how-it-works/ccpa-dark-patterns-warning-advisory-goes-out-to-streamers-fintechs-in-addition-to-e-commerce-firms-69331.htm)

### Inferences

- Label every ranked list that includes paid partners (for example "We may earn a commission; rankings…"), and disclose whether ranking is affected. Avoid "best card for you" if the ranking is commission-weighted, because that risks misleading-ad and "disguised advertisement" claims. In-app push notifications promoting cards need opt-in consent. Aaj doesn't need DLT registration if it sends no SMS or calls itself.

## 8. Tax: GST and TDS on affiliate commission; "marketplace" triggers

### Takeaway

Referral commissions are taxable services, generally at 18% GST. The usual services threshold is ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special-category states), but whether compulsory registration applies to "agents" is disputed. Payers deduct TDS on commission, which is now 2% with a ₹20,000 threshold under s.194H (the provision continues under the Income-tax Act 2025). Needs a CA.

### Cited Findings

- s.194H TDS cut from 5% to 2% from 1 Oct 2024. Threshold raised to ₹20,000 for FY2025-26. — [TaxBuddy](https://www.taxbuddy.com/blog/section-194h-tds-rate-in-2025-detailed-explanation); [RSM India](https://www.rsm.global/india/sites/default/files/media/publications/2025/RSM%20India%20Newsflash%20-%20Overview%20of%20Changes%20in%20TDS%20and%20TCS%20provisions%20w.e.f.%201st%20April%202025.pdf). Motilal Oswal states 10%, which conflicts and looks outdated: [Motilal Oswal](https://www.motilaloswal.com/personal-finance/tax/section-194h-of-income-tax-act-tds-on-commission-and-brokerage)
- The Income-tax Act 2025 carries over the 194H-type regime largely unchanged. — [ITA converter blog](https://ita1961to2025.pages.dev/blog/corporate-compliance/194h-return)
- GST: 18% on commissions from Indian affiliate programs. The registration threshold is ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh special-category states), counting all income. — [Cuelinks](https://www.cuelinks.com/blog/impact-gst-bloggers-affiliate-marketers-india/); [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/learn/gst-on-brokerage-and-commission/). Conflicting view: commission agents need compulsory registration under CGST s.24(vii) regardless of turnover — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=686425)

### Inferences

- No separate licence is triggered just by earning card referral fees. Becoming an insurance "web aggregator" or "corporate agent" (IRDAI) or a loan LSP would bring in separate regimes. Avoid insurance and loan listings unless licensed or partnered.

### Gaps

- No CBIC primary source was found. Note that s.24(vii) on its face concerns agents supplying on behalf of a principal, which arguably doesn't fit pure referral fees (CA to confirm). IRDAI web-aggregator rules were not researched.
