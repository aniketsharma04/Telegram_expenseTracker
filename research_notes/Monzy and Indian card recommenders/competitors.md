# Indian credit-card recommendation, comparison and reward-optimisation landscape (as of 1 Nov 2026)

Scope note: about 18 searches/fetches. Many players on the brief (CardExpert, TechnoFino card finder, Card Mavens, MyMoneyMantra, Wishfin, IndiaLends, Jupiter Edge, OneCard, Uni, Slice, POP, Fibe, Bajaj Markets, Paytm, PhonePe/GPay offers, Jar, Fi, INDmoney, Axio/Walnut, Money View) could **not** be checked inside the tool budget. They are listed under Gaps, not described from memory. Nothing was signed up for and no personal data was entered.

## Competitor map: who does what, how they recommend, how they monetise, scale

### Takeaway

The market splits into four groups. (1) Large lead-gen marketplaces: PaisaBazaar and BankBazaar, which earn per card issued, run bureau-score funnels, and in BankBazaar's case now earn mostly from co-branded cards. (2) Affiliate content and comparison sites and apps: CardInsider, Great.Cards, BankKaro/EarnKaro, which earn per approved card. (3) Issuer-partner fintechs with their own co-branded cards: CRED, Scapia, Kiwi. (4) A small, newer group of subscription "reward optimiser" apps: SaveSage, PointsFly, Credeem, ccreward, Cusp. Only the fourth group comes close to Kudos or CardPointers.

### Inferences

- Great.Cards appears to be a CashKaro/BankKaro-linked front end (gift card tracked on CashKaro Wallet), so it is affiliate-funded, not independent, despite lacking an explicit disclosure. This is inferred from the homepage only.
- BankBazaar now earns mostly from co-branded cards (about 84% of FY25 revenue by my calculation from the CEO's Rs 210 Cr figure), so its "comparison" carries a strong incentive to push its own co-brands. CRED, Scapia and Kiwi have the same structural incentive.
- Per-transaction "best card for this purchase" optimisation exists in India only in early-stage, subscription-priced apps (PointsFly, ccreward, Credeem, Cusp, plus SaveSage at the advisory end). None has public scale beyond SaveSage's self-reported figures.

## Spend-data-based recommendation vs questionnaire; per-transaction optimisation in India

### Takeaway

Most Indian players recommend from a questionnaire or category-spend inputs, or from a bureau score (marketplaces). Spend-data claims come from SaveSage ("spending patterns"), Great.Cards, FINQY and Credeem, but none of the pages checked say whether the data comes from SMS, email statements or Account Aggregator. CRED is the clearest Account Aggregator user, but for money management, not card recommendation. Per-purchase "which card" optimisation is limited to new apps (PointsFly AIRA, ccreward, Cusp, Credeem).

### Cited Findings

- SaveSage: "analyses your spending patterns across 1L+ data points to recommend the best cards". The ingestion method is not stated on the site. — [savesage.club](https://www.savesage.club/)
- PointsFly's AIRA chooses the card per purchase. — [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/pointsfly)
- ccreward "calculating and comparing rewards for every transaction". — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/ccreward/id6736835206)
- A vendor site (cardtrail) claims that "as of early 2026, no mainstream Indian app offers real-time 'use this card here' recommendations" and advises a manual cheat sheet. This conflicts with PointsFly and ccreward existing, but is consistent if "mainstream" means at scale. — [cardtrail](https://cardtrail.pages.dev/calculators/credit-card-spend-category-optimizer-india)
- The Account Aggregator framework can carry credit-card data, since most issuers sit inside banks (SBI Card and BoB Cards are exceptions), and every request needs explicit consent. — [Setu AA playbook](https://setu.co/AA_playbook.pdf); [IDFC FIRST](https://www.idfcfirstbank.com/finfirst-blogs/savings-account/what-is-account-aggregation)
- CRED Money analyses AA-sourced transactions. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/kunal-shah-led-cred-unveils-new-offering-to-help-customers-manage-bank-accounts-track-expenses)
- FINQY Test My Card matches on "spending patterns, income, and creditworthiness". — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/finqy-transforms-credit-card-selection-with-test-my-card-launch)

### Inferences

- An on-device SMS/email parser feeding both "which card to get" and "which card to use now" appears unclaimed at scale in India. A product that is free or ad-free, not dependent on affiliate links, and transparent about its data would stand out from the affiliate-led incumbents.

### Gaps

- Exact data-ingestion method (SMS, Gmail parsing, AA, statement upload) for SaveSage, Great.Cards, Credeem and PointsFly could not be confirmed from sources.
- Whether Axio/Walnut, Fi, Jupiter or INDmoney use SMS/AA spend data for card recommendations was not researched.

## Bureau data for pre-qualification and eligibility

### Takeaway

Bureau-based "pre-approved" offers are the marketplaces' core funnel (PaisaBazaar explicitly), but the licence or partner route (credit-information company membership vs bureau partnership) was not confirmed. Users widely distrust the "pre-approved" label.

### Cited Findings

- Paisabazaar: offers depend mainly on credit score, are not guaranteed, viewing is a soft check, and applying is a hard enquiry. Scores from CIBIL and other bureaus are offered free. — [Paisabazaar](https://www.paisabazaar.com/credit-score/do-pre-approved-credit-card-offers-affect-your-credit-score-aio/); [Paisabazaar app listing](https://cafebazaar.ir/app/com.paisabazaar?l=en)
- Forum disputes on whether pre-approval is genuine (mixed: some say "most probably you will get the card"). — [TechnoFino](https://technofino.in/community/threads/misleading-paisabazaar-offers.21405/latest)
- CRED Svalbard includes credit-score tools. — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/02/cred-unveils-svalbard-simplify-credit-management-boost-scores-kunal-shah)

### Gaps

- Which bureau licence or partner each player uses (for example Paisabazaar/BankBazaar with Experian/CIBIL/Equifax/CRIF, CRED with Experian) was not found in sources this round.
- Whether any small optimiser (SaveSage, PointsFly) does an eligibility check: not found.

## Business models, disclosure practices and gaps (bias, stale data, spam)

### Takeaway

Monetisation is overwhelmingly per-issued-card affiliate or DSA-style commission (Paisabazaar, BankKaro/EarnKaro, CardInsider, Great.Cards), increasingly co-branded card economics (BankBazaar, CRED, Scapia, Kiwi), and in a few cases consumer subscriptions (SaveSage, PointsFly, global Kudos/CardPointers/MaxRewards). Disclosure is weak, and spam calls after credit checks are a recurring complaint.

### Cited Findings

- Affiliate per-issued-card payouts of about Rs 1,750-2,000 (EarnKaro marketing). — [EarnKaro](https://earnkaro.com/blog/idfc-bank-affiliate-program/)
- Great.Cards has no visible compensation disclosure but runs a CashKaro gift-card incentive. — [great.cards](https://great.cards/)
- SaveSage calls itself "independent" with no commercial disclosure on the homepage. — [savesage.club](https://www.savesage.club/)
- A competitor, CardAdvisor, publishes a "how we make money" page (fixed fee from an affiliate network on approval). This is an example of better disclosure practice. — [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/how-we-make-money)
- Spam calls: an IANS report (Aug 2025) says CIBIL checks triggered "a flood of spam calls" from lenders including Bajaj Finance and PaisaBazaar. A TechnoFino user reports "a LOT of spam calls and SMSs" after use. — [IANS](https://ianslive.in/cibils-role-under-scanner-users-complain-of-spam-calls-after-credit-checks-on-bajaj-finance--20250822150135); [Lokmat Times](https://www.lokmattimes.com/business/cibils-role-under-scanner-users-complain-of-spam-calls-after-credit-checks-on-bajaj-finance/amp)
- Incentive structure: The Points Guy model shows affiliate fees dominate revenue in this category. — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/points-guy-built-business-based-affiliate-fees)

### Inferences

- The main gaps an independent product could exploit: (a) rankings that may favour the highest payouts or own co-brands (BankBazaar, CRED), (b) no real eligibility check behind "pre-approved", (c) spam and lead resale after score checks, (d) per-transaction optimisation available only in tiny paid apps, (e) global tools (CardPointers) do not support Indian cards.

### Gaps

- No primary evidence was found that any specific site orders rankings by payout. The bias claim is structural, not proven.
- Stale reward data (devaluations not reflected) is a commonly cited complaint, but no sourced example was collected this round.
- DSA registration status of each player was not verified.
- Reddit r/CreditCardsIndia and r/IndiaInvestments threads were not retrieved by the search tool.
