## Full description (4,000 characters max)

₹1,00,000 a month feels like plenty. Then a coffee, a Blinkit order, a Swiggy dinner and an Amazon parcel quietly
add up, and the month runs out before the salary does.

Aaj turns your monthly income into one number you can actually feel: what you can spend today.

**Your number, every morning**
Tell the app your income, your fixed costs (rent, EMI, SIP, subscriptions) and what you want to save. It works out
your everyday money and splits it across the days left in the month. Each morning you get one notification: today
you can spend ₹1,410.

**Tracked automatically**
No typing. When your bank or UPI app sends a payment alert, the app logs it and updates today's number within
seconds: "Blinkit −₹400, ₹810 left today". Rent and EMIs are recognised as fixed costs and don't eat into your day.

**Big purchases, handled**
Bought a ₹12,000 phone? It's spread across the rest of the month, or you can mark it as paid from savings in one
tap.

**A night report**
Every evening: what you spent, what's left for tomorrow, and whether you're on track for your savings goal.

**Plan the month, and what's ahead**

- Budget months that start on your salary day, not the 1st
- Goals like a trip or an emergency fund, credited automatically each month
- Category limits with alerts at 80% and 100%
- Bills and reminders before they're due
- Spending insights by category and merchant
- Family view to see the household's spending together

**Private by design**
The app only reads notifications that mention money moving, and messages with OTPs or codes are skipped on your
phone and never sent. No ads, no selling data. Export or delete everything at any time.

Works in English and Hindi.
